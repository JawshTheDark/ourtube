OurTube for Android 1.8.0: groups, and Share → OurTube.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your phone or tablet and open it. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your servers and sign-ins.

**Groups** (needs OurTube 1.24 on your server): share videos with just your family or a few friends.

- **You → Groups:** make a group, add people by username or send them the join link, rename or delete it, or leave one you were added to.
- **Only for a group** is a new visibility in Upload and in a video's details. Those videos stay off Home, search and the network, and show on the group's page.
- Group join links open in the app, and so do group notifications. Watch together is hidden for group videos.
- **Upload** links to the website's import page, for bringing videos over from YouTube or Immich.

**Views count once you've watched most of a video** (75%), as on the website from OurTube 1.25, for videos, Shorts and videos from other servers. Opening a video still adds it to your history. Update the app before your server moves to 1.25, or the views you make in it stop counting.

**Share → OurTube** (new since 1.7.0): share a video from Gallery, Files, Google Photos or Immich and pick OurTube. Upload opens with the video chosen and the title filled in. In Immich, use **Share**, not **Share link**, which sends only a link.
