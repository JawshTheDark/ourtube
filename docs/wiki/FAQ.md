# FAQ: where is it?

The questions people ask most, each with where to click and a link to the full story.

Paths start from what's on the screen. The **sidebar** is the menu on the left; on a phone or a narrow window it's
behind the ☰ button at the top left, or shrunk to icons. The **account menu** opens when you click your picture at
the top right. **Settings** and, for admins, **Admin** are in the account menu at every window size.

## A few answers first

**I'm the admin and I can't find the Admin page, or Invites.** Click **Admin** (the shield) at the bottom of the
sidebar, or click your picture at the top right, then **Admin**. Before 1.25.2 the slim sidebar of a laptop-sized
window left **Admin** out, so on an older server use your picture. **Invites** is a box on the **Overview** tab, below the **Users** table: scroll down. See
[Inviting people](Admin-Guide.md#inviting-people).

**Do I have to write rules or terms?** No. A server for friends works without any. If you want them, write them in
**Admin → Legal**: rules and contacts appear on the server's `/legal` page, and terms become a box people tick
when they sign up. See [Writing rules and terms](Admin-Guide.md#writing-rules-and-terms).

**How do people join an invite-only server?** An admin makes an invite link in **Admin → Overview → Invites** and
sends it. Opening the link fills in the code on the sign-up form. Members can't make invite links. See
[How sign-up works here](Getting-Started.md#how-sign-up-works-here).

**Can I change my username?** No, it's your channel's address and stays the same. Your display name, picture and
description can change any time in **Settings → Channel**. See
[What isn't there](Accounts-and-Settings.md#what-isnt-there).

**I forgot my password.** **Sign in → Forgot your password?** sends a reset link, but only to an email address
you confirmed in **Settings → Notifications → Email**. Without one, the admin has to help; see
[When someone forgets their password](Admin-Guide.md#when-someone-forgets-their-password).

**Nobody can see my new upload.** It's a draft until you click **Save details**. See
[Drafts](Uploading-and-Editing.md#drafts-nothing-goes-out-until-you-save).

**My view count didn't go up.** A view counts once three quarters of the video were played, once per viewer every
six hours. See [What counts as a view](Watching-and-Discovery.md#what-counts-as-a-view).
